Building a website with me

This is the whole agreement. If anything here is unclear, ask me before you pay anything.

What it costs

ServiceStarting price
One-page sitefrom $100
Multi-page sitefrom $250

These are starting prices, not final ones. Bigger jobs cost more. I will always give you one fixed number in writing before any work begins, and that number does not change unless you ask for something that was not in the original plan.

Paying

  • Half of the agreed price up front. I start once it arrives.
  • The other half before the site goes live.
  • We agree on how you are paying by email before you send anything.

I am in 8th grade, so payments are handled through a parent's account. You will get the details in writing before you send anything.

Changes

  • You can send me a list of changes twice before the site goes live. Both are included in the price.
  • Send each list all at once so I can do them together.
  • After those two lists I will still make changes. I will tell you what they cost before I start.
  • If you decide you want a different site than the one we agreed on, that is a new job and needs a new price.

How long it takes

I give you an estimate before starting. I am in school, so most of the work happens after school and on weekends. If something is going to be late I will tell you before the deadline, not after it.

Your domain and hosting

  • You buy the domain and the hosting yourself, in your own name, on your own card. A domain is usually about $12 a year.
  • A plain site can normally be hosted for free. One with accounts or a database costs a few dollars a month, and I will tell you the exact amount before we start.
  • You renew the domain yourself each year. If it expires, the site stops loading until you renew it.
  • I will tell you exactly what to buy, so you do not pay for extras you do not need.

Who owns it

  • Once the final payment clears, the code is yours to keep, change, or give to someone else.
  • I do not charge anything monthly and I do not keep any part of the code.
  • I would like to show the finished site in my portfolio. If you would rather I did not, say so and I will not.

If it does not work out

  • If you cancel after I have started, the deposit covers the work already done.
  • If I cannot finish for any reason, I refund the deposit in full and you owe me nothing.

What I do not do

I do not write your text or take your photos, though I am happy to say what is needed. I do not run ads, manage social media, or maintain the site month to month unless we agree that separately.
